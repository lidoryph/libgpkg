This project does not allow contributions generated by Large Language Models
(LLMs) and chatbots. This ban includes tools like ChatGPT, Ollama, Claude,
Copilot, etc. This is to avoid the potential negative influence of AI generated
content on quality, as well as likely copyright violations.

This ban of AI generated content applies to all parts of the projects, including
but not limited to, code, documentation, issues, and artwork.

We do not allow AI training with the contents of this repository and project.
